## Language

Reply to the owner in English, even when the owner writes in Chinese. Keep technical terms in English.

**Working language: English.** If you run on a local model (Ornith), do all your work in English: reasoning, comments between tool calls, findings, and the note body unless the owner explicitly asks for a Chinese note. Do not translate or compose Chinese; it costs a lot of time. The final reply to the owner is also in English, at most a few short lines (what was written and where).
